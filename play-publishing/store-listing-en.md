# Google Play listing — English

**Title (29/30):** SecureVault: Passwords & Docs

**Short description (max 80 characters):**  
Encrypted vault for passwords, docs and files. Purchased Pro works offline.

**Full description:**

SecureVault is a local, encrypted vault for passwords, documents, images and files. Your
data stays in an encrypted vault on your device; the app does not operate an account or
its own cloud service.

**Passwords and codes**
- Store logins, passwords, URLs, notes, tags and TOTP codes.
- Search entries, organize them in folders and mark favorites.
- Use the password generator and local checks for weak or reused passwords.
- Unlock with strong biometrics or your Android screen-lock PIN, pattern or password.
  Biometrics are optional, but the device must have a secure screen lock.
- Enable Android Autofill or Credential Manager to select a saved login after device
  authentication.

**Encrypted vault**
- VLT3 encrypts vault data locally.
- Recovering on a new device requires both your master passphrase and a separate
  Recovery QR. Store them separately.
- Create encrypted backups at a location you choose. The copy is refreshed after a
  successful save; it is not a background schedule.
- Add images, file attachments and multi-page document scans as encrypted vault data.

**SecureVault Pro**
Pro unlocks password history, file attachments, document scanning and advanced
import/export. Pro is unlocked with a one-time Google Play purchase. You can restore
the purchase in Settings. The last confirmed entitlement is cached on this device so
Pro remains available offline.

SecureVault does not sync your vault to a publisher server. You choose where to store
backups. Exporting an individual attachment or CSV can write decrypted data to the
destination you select.

Protect your master passphrase and Recovery QR. Do not store them together.
